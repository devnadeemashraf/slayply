Privacy

Stored on your device

Your profile, resumes, saved jobs, answer history and API keys live only in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local). Slayply has no accounts, analytics or telemetry, and this site sets no cookies.

Sent when you ask for a draft

The text needed for the draft (job description, questions, relevant profile facts and the resume you chose) goes through the Slayply relay to the provider you picked: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. The relay uses your API key for that one request and does not log or store the key or the content. It keeps only short-lived, in-memory data: your IP address for rate limiting and a fingerprint of your key to cache the provider's model list for ten minutes. In Direct Mode (Claude only) the request goes straight from your browser to Anthropic and skips the relay.

Your provider's policy applies

The provider handles what it receives under your agreement with it. Some provider tiers, often free or consumer plans, may train on what you send. Use a plan whose terms exclude training before you send anything personal.

Reading job pages

Slayply reads a page only when you ask it to, by clicking the toolbar icon, using the context menu or pasting text. On LinkedIn it reads only text you select. If you switch on "Capture on popular job sites" and capture a Greenhouse job, it asks Greenhouse's public Job Board API for that job's questions using only the board name and job number already in the page address.

Deleting your data

Settings has an export, an import and a button that wipes every local store and your key.

Payments and minors

Slayply takes no payments and collects no personal data from anyone, including minors.
